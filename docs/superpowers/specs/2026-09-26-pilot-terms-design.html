Pilot terms: T3 + T4 mockup

One screen a coach accepts once, before a team workspace is created. Built from the real claim-flow shell (ClaimShell, 720 column), the join flow's terms rows (TermMark) and DS tokens. Hours and the end date come from config, so the college and custom-team screens differ only where the tier does.

College: the terms come after the email link because that is the first point the coach is signed in. Custom: after the team is named, so the eyebrow can carry the name.

Decisions in this frame

  • Term 3 is the only one that asks the coach to state something, so it is a checkbox. The other four are facts to read, drawn with the join flow's TermMark.
  • Blue marks = what the program gets (1, 2). Grey marks = what we reserve (4, 5). Same pairing as the join terms.
  • The checkbox sits outside the mark list, so no blue checks stack above a blue checkbox (the TermMark rule).
  • 75h and "Dec 31, 2026" come from getMonthlyCapHours and formatPilotEnd.
  • Term 3 wording is pending legal review.

Handoff · Video fullscreen — phase 2 of 2
The room: a board, a court, a transport, and one list that comes when called.
The fullscreen half of the Video tab. It is a portal at document root, not a route — it opens over the report from the transport's expand control or the feed's "Open fullscreen", carrying the current point and the applied cut with it. Everything on the dark scope is one of four objects: the FilmBoard (the rail's scoreboard, movable), the FilmCourt (a readout, never a control), the FilmTransportBar, and the points drawer — which is the phase-1 point list on a dark tone, not a second list. Rule This is the rail's scoreboard on the dark scope — one component draws the number a player reads paused in the room and the number they read in the report. The point it names now lives inside the slab, under the hairline, so nothing floats loose on the film. The set, the game and who serves are already in the cells and the dot, so they are never said twice. Survives the chrome collapse at 82% opacity. tabindex=0, arrow keys nudge 8px (⇧ 40px). C2FilmCourt214 × 350
Handoff notes
Layout 214px, 10px pad, 8px radius, rgba(13,13,13,.8). 22px header (11/500 title + two 22px glyph buttons), 178×266 court, then a legend row: two 7px key dots at 10px/60% white, mono count right. Court lines rgba(255,255,255,.24), outer .34, net .6, surface rgba(214,228,249,.07) — full court, both halves, net across the middle. Rule Point mode is the rally as it happens: a donut where the ball was struck, a filled dot where it landed, both fading at 1 → 0.5 → 0.22 and gone two shots later, so the court never accumulates into a chart. The live bounce carries an 0.85 white ring. Match mode plots bounces only, 5px, and follows the applied cut — 14 points under a cut, not 174. Behavior A readout, not a control: it sits in the left column under the board, is not draggable, and the drawer never displaces it. Every mark is a seek target; hover opens the product's dark readout (chart-tooltip.tsx, 168px, 12px radius). Survives the chrome collapse, losing only its two header glyphs (controls=false). Off is a transport toggle, not a close button on the card. Data Props: mode="point"|"match"|"none" · title · caption · activeShot · controls · readout. Colors are chart-only literals: you #60A5FA, opponent #94A3B8, out #E5484D. Coordinates are percentages of the court box, so one geometry serves every size.
C3FilmTransportBarfull width · 124 tall
Handoff notes
Layout Three stacked rows in padding:0 24px 14px, 9px apart, over the frame's own bottom scrim (linear-gradient(to bottom, … rgba(13,13,13,.78) 100%) — the bar draws no surface of its own). Row 1: 12/500 match title + 10px context left, mono position + two 14px point chevrons right. Row 2: the 16px seek lane. Row 3: 40px control row, 18px gaps — play · skip-back · skip-forward · mono time · flex · bookmark · dead-time · speed · loop · sound · court · exit · more. Seek lane 3px track, pill radius, segmented per set with 5px gaps: played --blue, unplayed rgba(255,255,255,.22), the in-progress set a gradient at the play head. 11px white knob. role="slider", keyboard-seekable; under a cut the lane still represents the whole film — the cut governs point stepping, never the timeline. Controls 15px Lucide at stroke 1.6, 85% white resting, white active. Every one gets an aria-label and a dark tooltip naming it plus its key — no bare icon buttons. State lives in the label, not a second glyph: "Sound — on", "Loop this point — off", "Show the court — on". Bookmark is the one filled glyph when a point is saved; the court glyph dims to 45% when the court is off. "More" is present at 45% and inert until it has a menu — it is the only disabled control allowed here. Behavior The whole bar is chrome: it collapses after 3s of stillness while playing and returns on any pointer move, key or focus (200ms opacity, no transform). It never collapses while paused, while a menu is open, or while focus is inside it. With the drawer open the bar's right edge stops at the drawer (right:320px) rather than sliding under it. Opening state: no board, no court, position and duration read —.
Phase 2 · B — Every state of the room
Eleven states, one composition.
Left column: board, then court beneath it. Right: the points drawer when called. Foot: the transport. Nothing else is ever on screen. Frames are at 1280 × 720; the composition is the same at any size because every slab is inset from an edge, not positioned on a grid.

Phase 2 · C — Keys and tooltips
Every control has a name and a key.
The room is icon-only, so the dark tooltip is what gives each glyph its label back. Every entry below is the tooltip string and the key in one — never a native title, never a bare glyph.

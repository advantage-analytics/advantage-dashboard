The feed answers the rail Collapsing the icon rail (⌘\) returns 168px to the content column, and the shot feed is the only thing that spends it. The point list never collapses — it is how you move through the match, and 320px is what a two-line row costs.
Rail open # · Player · Stroke · Placement · Result. Type and Spin drop — Type restates the row's position, and Spin is a qualifier nobody scans a column for. Stroke names itself: Serve on row 1, then the bare stroke. Placement stays, because it is the tactical payload and the reason to read the feed at all. Rail collapsed # · Player · Spin · Stroke · Type · Placement · Mph · Result. The added columns sit in the order a player says them — "slice backhand return, cross court, out" — so the row reads as a sentence rather than a schema. Rules Columns are added and removed, never re-sorted: #, Player, Stroke, Placement and Result hold the same x in both states, so nothing a coach has learned to find moves. Placement takes the one fluid track either way. Mph is tabular, right, and absent — —, never 0 — where nothing was measured. Discovery This is a reward, not a feature to advertise: the rail toggle keeps its own label and tooltip and says nothing about columns. Nothing in the product is reachable only through the wide feed — every value here is also in the point's own record.

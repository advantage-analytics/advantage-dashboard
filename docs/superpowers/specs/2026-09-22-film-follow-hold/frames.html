Design · Video tab — the film points follow playback, or hold
Touch the list and it stays where you are reading; one pill takes you back to the film.
Both the room's points drawer (film-room-drawer.tsx → PointList tone="dark") and the shell's "This point" card (film-this-point.tsx) track the playing point. This frame adds one shared state, pointFocus: follow | held(pointId). The well and the card hold; the lit data-playing row, the scoreboard, the court, the position counter and the room's court title keep following. No second well, no accordion, nothing persisted, playback untouched. Sample match throughout: Jordan Lee v Marcus Reid, an 87-point cut of a 174-point film; the viewer is reading point 9 while the film plays point 14.

The pill is the "Points" trigger's recipe (film-fullscreen.tsx l.1509–1536): 28px, rgba(13,13,13,0.72) → 0.9 on hover, px-2.5, 11px/500 white, --radius-button, the same 200ms --ease-primary colour transition — plus the drawer's own 1px 10% inset hairline so it reads over the lit row as well as over the sheet. Centred, bottom: 12px, inside the positioning wrapper the design adds around the scroller (not inside it, or it would scroll away). It carries data-film-chrome, so the R2 collapse and the exit fade (l.926–934) take it with the rest of the chrome. Chevron is Lucide at 12px: chevron-down when the lit row is below the viewport's centre, chevron-up when above. The pill never covers the held well: the wrapper pins it to the list's bottom edge and the well sits wherever the viewer left it — a well that runs under the pill is the viewer's own scroll position, and one wheel tick clears it. Press → follow: the list scrolls smoothly to point 14, its well unfolds, the pill exits (100ms opacity).

Holding is the absence of a scroll, and it needs no animation of its own

Reduced motion, in one line per surface

  • Drawer: re-follow lands on the playing row at once; the pill fades in 150ms, out 100ms; the well is at full height from its first frame and fades (as it does today).
  • Card: the rows swap without the stagger (film-shot-row-in is animation: none); the header line fades in and out, no rise.
  • Nothing is removed from the reduced-motion path that carries state: the line and the pill still appear, the lit row still moves, the well still opens.

Keyboard and screen reader

  • The pill is a <button> inside aside[aria-label="Points"], in DOM order after the scroller — Tab reaches it after the last row; it is never in the tab order while hidden (unmounted, not opacity 0).
  • aria-label="Now playing: point 14 — follow playback" on both the pill and the header line; the visible string stays the short one.
  • The lit row's data-playing="true" and its aria-current keep naming the playing point while held; the held row gets no attribute of its own — its open well is the mark.
  • Focus on the pill holds the room's chrome up (focusHolds), like focus on any other chrome control.

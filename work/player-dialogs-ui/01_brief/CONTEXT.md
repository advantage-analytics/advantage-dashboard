# Stage 01 — Brief

## Inputs
- working: `../BRIEF-SEED.md` (the human's raw intent, any form)
- `references/` — anything the human dropped there

## Process
Refine the seed into a brief a designer could work from: goal, scope,
non-goals, constraints, success criteria, open questions. Keep the human's
intent — sharpen it, don't grow it. If the seed is ambiguous on something
material, ask in chat before writing; put what stays unresolved under "Open
questions" rather than guessing. Do not propose solutions here — that is
stage 02's job.

## Outputs
- `output/brief.md` — sections: Goal · Scope · Non-goals · Constraints ·
  Success criteria · Open questions

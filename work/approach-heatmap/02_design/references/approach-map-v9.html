Three ways to cut an approach

  • How you came in: approach, serve and volley, or drawn in, from netEntryOf. The card opens on approaches; the other two are one tick away in Filters.
  • Approach to their forehand, middle or backhand is the coach's question. The lanes are fixed on the court; only the words depend on the opponent's hand.
  • Direction (crosscourt, middle, down the line) is recorded on 92.6% of shots and needs no hand. A serve has none, so serve and volley points sit outside it.

Gaps for the brief

  • The reply is the opponent's deciding shot on a point you lost. Line or crosscourt comes from its direction field.
  • Lobs come from ball height, not yet checked on a labelled match. SwingVision imports have no clips.
  • Across matches, the same card on a player's season page puts every match's approaches on one court.
